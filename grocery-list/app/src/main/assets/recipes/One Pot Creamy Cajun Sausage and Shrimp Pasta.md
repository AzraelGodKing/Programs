# One Pot Creamy Cajun Sausage & Shrimp Pasta

**Source:** [Facebook reel](https://www.facebook.com/reel/39054906504100148/) (shared via https://www.facebook.com/share/r/1cbVFmnhgU/). The official written recipe is the paid Substack post [One-Pot Creamy Cajun Sausage & Shrimp Pasta — Seafood Network](https://seafoodnetwork.substack.com/p/one-pot-creamy-cajun-sausage-and).

**Servings:** 4 *(estimate)*
**Prep time:** 10 min *(estimate)*
**Cook time:** 25–30 min *(estimate)*

## Ingredients
- Oil, for the pan *(amount not shown)*
- 1 lb sliced sausage, it looks like andouille (the pan looks closer to about 13 oz) *(amount and the exact sausage type are estimates)*
- ½ large onion, sliced *(estimate)*
- 1 red bell pepper, diced *(estimate)*
- 1 can (14.5 oz) Hunt’s Fire Roasted Diced tomatoes *(the Hunt’s fire-roasted diced tomatoes are shown; the can size is an estimate)*
- Garlic *(amount not shown)*
- 2–3 cups broth, poured from a measuring cup *(amount is an estimate; a measuring cup is shown)*
- 8 oz dry rigatoni, added straight from the box *(estimate)*
- About 1 cup heavy cream *(estimate)*
- 4 oz cream cheese, from a block *(estimate)*
- Cajun seasoning *(amount not shown; estimate: 1–2 tbsp, to taste)*
- ½ cup shredded Parmesan *(estimate)*
- About ½ lb shrimp, peeled and deveined *(estimate; cooked shrimp are shown at the end)*
- Fresh parsley, for garnish *(shown)*

## Steps
1. Heat the oil in the deep skillet over medium to medium-high heat *(heat is an estimate)*. Add the sliced sausage and brown it, about 4–5 min *(time is an estimate)*. Move it to a plate.
2. Add the sliced onion and diced red bell pepper. Cook until they start to soften, 3–4 min *(time is an estimate)*.
3. Stir in the fire-roasted diced tomatoes with their juice, then the garlic. Cook about 1 min *(time is an estimate)*.
4. Pour in the broth and add the dry rigatoni. Season with Cajun seasoning. Bring to a simmer and cook, stirring often, until the pasta is tender and the sauce is thick and orange, 12–15 min *(time is an estimate)*. If the pan gets dry before the pasta is done, add a splash more broth.
5. Stir in the heavy cream and the cream cheese until the cheese melts and the sauce is creamy.
6. Stir in the shredded Parmesan, then add the sausage back to the pan.
7. Add the shrimp. *(the reel only shows cooked shrimp at the end, and it doesn’t show when they went in; adding cooked shrimp at the end is an estimate so they don’t overcook. If you’re starting with raw shrimp, sear them after the sausage and set them aside, or nestle them in for the last few minutes of the simmer — both of those are estimates.)*
8. Finish with chopped parsley and serve.

## Tips
- The official amounts, step-by-step instructions, and chef’s tips are in Seafood Network’s paid Substack post: [One-Pot Creamy Cajun Sausage & Shrimp Pasta](https://seafoodnetwork.substack.com/p/one-pot-creamy-cajun-sausage-and). Use that if you have access; everything measured here is only an estimate from the video.
- The public Substack preview matches what the reel shows: smoky Cajun sausage, shrimp, fire-roasted tomatoes, rigatoni cooked in the broth, and a sauce made with cream cheese, heavy cream, and Parmesan.
- Andouille will give you more smoke and heat than a mild smoked sausage.
- Taste before you add much salt. Cajun seasoning, fire-roasted tomatoes, broth, sausage, and Parmesan are all salty on their own.
