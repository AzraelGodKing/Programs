# Steak Tortellini (DASH)

**Based on:** [[Steak Tortellini]]
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 10 min · **Cook:** 30 min

> DASH swaps: 12 oz of lean sirloin instead of 1½ lb of steak (3 oz a serving, inside DASH's lean-meat limit), a salt-free Cajun blend, no butter or Velveeta, 1% milk thickened with cornstarch instead of heavy cream, low-sodium beef broth, one 9 oz package of tortellini instead of 18 oz, and mushrooms and spinach to fill it out.

## Ingredients

**For the steak**
- 12 oz sirloin steak, trimmed and cut into bite-size pieces
- 2 tbsp avocado oil, divided

**For the pasta and sauce**
- 9 oz cheese tortellini
- 8 oz mushrooms, sliced
- 5 cloves garlic, minced, divided
- 1 cup low-sodium beef broth
- 1½ cups 1% milk
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- ⅓ cup grated Parmesan
- 4 cups baby spinach
- Cracked black pepper, to taste

**Salt-free Cajun blend (about 2 tbsp; most for the steak, a teaspoon for the sauce)**
- 2 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ¼ tsp cayenne

## Steps
1. Mix the Cajun blend. Toss the steak with 1 tbsp avocado oil and all but 1 tsp of the blend.
2. Heat a large pan over medium-high with ½ tbsp avocado oil. Sear the steak undisturbed 2 min, flip, and cook 2 min more (longer for more done). Add 2 cloves of the garlic for the last minute. Move to a plate and tent with foil.
3. Add the remaining oil and the mushrooms; cook until browned, 5 min. Add the remaining garlic for 30 seconds.
4. Pour in the beef broth and simmer 5 min, scraping up the browned bits.
5. Meanwhile, cook the tortellini in unsalted water per the package. Drain.
6. Whisk the cornstarch into the milk and stir it into the pan with the last 1 tsp of blend and pepper. Simmer gently until thickened, 2–3 min.
7. Off the heat, stir in the Parmesan, then the tortellini and spinach until wilted.
8. Top with the steak bites and serve.

## Tips
- Sear the steak in batches if the pan is crowded so it browns instead of steaming.
- Tortellini brands vary a lot in sodium; pick the lowest you can find, or use whole wheat cheese tortellini.
