# One Pot Creamy Cajun Sausage Pasta

**Source:** [Facebook reel](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1600865928144161/), shared via https://www.facebook.com/share/r/1ShFZyXkqJ/
**Creator:** Zachs.foods ("Recipes better than takeout," Ep. 16)
**Servings:** 4–6 *(estimate from how full the pan looks)*
**Prep time:** 10 min *(estimate)*
**Cook time:** 25–30 min *(estimate)*
**Pan:** a deep 10–12" straight-sided skillet

> The creator says the full recipe is "in my bio," but the reel itself gives no amounts. Every amount below is an estimate unless it's marked as shown.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil, split between two additions *(estimate)*
- 12–14 oz pre-cooked smoked sausage, sliced into rounds *(estimate; it looks like a chicken or pork sausage with peppers in it)*
- 1 medium white onion, diced *(estimate)*
- 1 red bell pepper, diced *(estimate)*
- 2 tbsp Cajun seasoning, homemade or store-bought; use about **half** in the pot, then add more to taste *(amount is an estimate; "about half" is from the reel)*
- 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced *(estimate)*
- 1 can (14.5 oz) diced tomatoes with their juice *(shown: S&W; the can size is an estimate)*
- 2–2½ cups beef broth *(shown poured from a measuring cup that looks like about 2 cups)*
- 8 oz dry rigatoni *(shown: Barilla rigatoni; about half the box is an estimate)*
- ¾–1 cup heavy cream *(estimate)*
- 4 oz cream cheese, about half a block *(estimate from the video)*
- ½ cup freshly grated Parmesan *(estimate)*
- 1 cup shredded cheese for the top, like a mozzarella and cheddar blend *(estimate)*
- Chopped fresh parsley for garnish *(shown)*

## Steps
1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in the skillet over medium-high. Add the sausage and cook until it's crispy on both sides, 4–5 min *(time is an estimate)*. Move it to a bowl.
2. Add a little more oil, then the onion and pepper. Cook until soft, 3–4 min *(estimate)*.
3. Stir in about half the Cajun seasoning, then the garlic. Cook 30 seconds.
4. Add the diced tomatoes with their juice and simmer for a few minutes.
5. Pour in the beef broth and add the dry rigatoni. Bring to a simmer and cook, stirring often, until most of the liquid is absorbed and the pasta is al dente, 12–15 min *(estimate)*. If it gets dry before the pasta is done, add a splash more broth.
6. Lower the heat. Stir in the heavy cream, cream cheese, and Parmesan until the sauce is smooth.
7. Put the sausage back in and stir. Taste, and add more Cajun seasoning if you want.
8. Sprinkle the shredded cheese over the top and let it melt, covered, for 1–2 min *(estimate)*. Garnish with parsley.

## Tips
- Store-bought Cajun seasoning is often salty, so taste before you add salt. Beef broth adds salt too.
- Andouille gives you more heat and a classic Cajun flavor.
- If you want to use the whole 16 oz box of pasta, use about 4 cups of broth and a little more cream *(estimate)*.
- Quick homemade Cajun blend *(not from the reel)*: 2 tsp paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp onion powder, ½ tsp oregano, ½ tsp thyme, ½ tsp black pepper, ¼–½ tsp cayenne, ½ tsp salt.
