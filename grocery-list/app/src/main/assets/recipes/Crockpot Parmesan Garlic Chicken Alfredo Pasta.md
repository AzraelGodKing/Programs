# Crockpot Parmesan Garlic Chicken Alfredo Pasta

**Source:** [Facebook reel by Cookinginthemidwest](https://www.facebook.com/reel/39080713498194070/) (shared as https://www.facebook.com/share/r/19YcjJd1eE/)
**Servings:** ~6 *(estimate)*
**Prep:** ~15 min *(estimate)* · **Cook:** about 6 hr on LOW, plus ~10 min to finish

> The reel has no written recipe. Only the chicken (1 lb), the jar of Alfredo, the box of pasta, and about 6 hours on low were stated. Everything else is measured from what's on screen or estimated.

## Ingredients
- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts (she uses 3 small breasts)
- 1–2 tbsp dry seasoning blend *(estimate; labels not shown. A good mix: 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp onion powder, 1 tsp Italian seasoning, ½ tsp salt, ½ tsp pepper)*
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- ⅓ cup Buffalo Wild Wings Parmesan Garlic sauce *(estimated from the video, ¼–½ cup)*
- 1 jar Bertolli Alfredo sauce (about 15 oz *(estimate)*)
- 1 box cavatappi pasta (Barilla shown; a standard 16 oz box *(estimate)*)
- 1½ cups shredded mozzarella *(stated as "as much as it takes", estimated)*
- Fresh parsley, chopped, for garnish
- Salt for the pasta water *(estimate)*

## Steps
1. Put the chicken in a 6 qt slow cooker. Sprinkle on the seasoning, add the minced garlic, zigzag the Parmesan Garlic sauce over the top, then pour in the Alfredo.
2. Cover and cook on LOW for about 6 hours, until the chicken is 165°F and tender.
3. About 20 minutes before the end, boil the cavatappi in salted water until al dente (check the box *(estimate)*). Drain.
4. Pull out the chicken, chop it into bite-size chunks on a cutting board, and return it to the pot.
5. Stir in the cooked pasta and the mozzarella until the cheese melts and the sauce coats everything. Let it sit covered a few minutes if the cheese needs help *(estimate)*.
6. Top with parsley. She serves it with garlic bread and a Caesar-style side salad.

## Tips
- A full pound of pasta can soak up a lot of sauce. If it looks dry, add a splash of milk or pasta water, or use a little less pasta.
- Undercook the pasta slightly if you're planning leftovers.
- Leftovers keep about 4 days in the fridge *(estimate, standard food safety)*.
