# Cajun Chicken Alfredo Soup (DASH)

**Based on:** [[Cajun Chicken Alfredo Soup]]
**Servings:** 6
**Prep:** 15 min · **Cook:** 30 min
**Pot:** a large Dutch oven

> DASH swaps: no bacon or butter (olive oil and smoked paprika instead), 1% milk in place of half and half, low-sodium broth, a salt-free Cajun blend, whole-wheat pasta, less cheese, and the Cajun "trinity" of onion, celery and bell pepper plus spinach for vegetables.

## Ingredients

**For the chicken**
- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breast, sliced
- 1 tbsp olive oil

**For the soup**
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 2 celery stalks, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 2 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ¼ cup all-purpose flour
- 4 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 3 cups 1% milk
- 8 oz whole wheat fettuccine, broken into bite-size pieces
- 3 cups baby spinach
- ⅓ cup grated Parmesan
- ¼ cup shredded part-skim mozzarella

**Salt-free Cajun blend (about 2 tbsp; half for the chicken, half for the soup)**
- 2 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp cayenne

## Steps
1. Mix the Cajun blend. Toss the chicken with 1 tbsp olive oil and half the blend.
2. Brown the chicken in the Dutch oven over medium heat until cooked through, about 8 min. Move it to a cutting board.
3. Add 1 tbsp olive oil, the onion, celery, and bell pepper. Cook until soft, 5–6 min. Add the garlic and smoked paprika and cook 1 min.
4. Stir in the flour and cook 2–3 min so it doesn't taste raw.
5. Add the broth, scraping up the browned bits, then the milk and the rest of the Cajun blend. Bring to a gentle simmer (don't boil hard, or the milk can split).
6. Add the pasta and simmer, stirring now and then, until tender, about 12–15 min.
7. Take it off the heat. Stir in the spinach, Parmesan, and mozzarella.
8. Chop the chicken, stir it back in, and taste. Add more Cajun blend or a squeeze of lemon before reaching for salt.

## Tips
- Serve with a green salad rather than garlic bread.
- The pasta keeps soaking up liquid; loosen leftovers with a splash of broth or milk.
