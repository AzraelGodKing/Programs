# One Pan Chicken Gnocchi with Sun-Dried Tomatoes (DASH)

**Based on:** [[One Pan Chicken Bacon Gnocchi]]
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 15 min · **Cook:** 25–30 min
**Pan:** a large skillet

> DASH swaps: no bacon or bacon grease (olive oil and smoked paprika instead), spice-rubbed cutlets instead of a Parmesan breading, 1% milk instead of heavy cream, less Parmesan, a smaller amount of gnocchi (store gnocchi is salty) stretched with zucchini, and more spinach.

## Ingredients
- 2 boneless skinless chicken breasts (about 1 lb), split into thin cutlets
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika, plus more for the sauce
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
- ¼ cup oil-packed sun-dried tomatoes, drained and chopped
- 12 oz potato gnocchi
- 1 zucchini, diced
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- ¾ cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 cup 1% milk
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- ¼ cup freshly shredded Parmesan
- 4 cups chopped spinach

## Steps
1. Mix the Italian seasoning, garlic powder, smoked paprika, and pepper and rub it over both sides of the cutlets.
2. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium. Cook the chicken 3 min per side until golden and cooked through. Set aside.
3. Add 1 tbsp olive oil, the sun-dried tomatoes, gnocchi, and zucchini. Toss until the gnocchi are toasted, 5–6 min. Stir in the garlic.
4. Whisk the cornstarch into the milk. Add the broth and the milk mixture with a pinch more smoked paprika. Simmer until it thickens and the gnocchi are tender, 3–4 min.
5. Stir in the Parmesan, then the spinach until wilted.
6. Slice the chicken and set it on top.

## Tips
- Brands of gnocchi vary a lot in sodium; check labels and pick one under about 300 mg a serving.
- Cauliflower gnocchi works too and adds a vegetable.
