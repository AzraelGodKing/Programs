# Creamy Garlic Chicken Parmesan Pasta

**Source:** [Facebook reel](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1889876468662191/) (shared via https://www.facebook.com/share/r/1CgYcVBtro/). The Facebook caption says to comment “recipe,” and the matching written recipe is on his shop: [Chicken Garlic Parmesan Pasta — Meatlicious](https://meatlicious.jupiter.shop/recipe/chicken-garlic-parmesan-pasta-5).
**Creator:** Meatlicious
**Servings:** 4 (from the Meatlicious recipe page)
**Prep time:** 15 min (from the Meatlicious recipe page)
**Cook time:** 25–30 min *(estimate from the steps: about 5 minutes per side to sear, 10–12 minutes covered, then the sauce)*

Amounts below are from his Meatlicious recipe page unless marked as an estimate. The page does not give amounts for the chicken seasoning blend.

## Ingredients
- 1½ lb chicken breast
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- Paprika, oregano, thyme, and black pepper, for seasoning the chicken *(no amounts listed; he says to season generously with this blend plus garlic powder, salt, and pepper)*
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 4 tbsp butter
- 2 tsp shallot *(the page doesn’t say fresh or dried)*
- 2 tsp dried Italian seasoning
- 2 tbsp plain flour
- ½ cup chicken broth
- 3 cups heavy cream
- 12 oz Parmesan cheese
- 1 roasted garlic *(optional; the steps say to mash it into the sauce if using — this is probably 1 head, but the page just says “1 roasted garlic”)*
- 12 oz pasta (the steps specify mafaldine)
- ¼ cup reserved pasta water, plus more as needed

## Steps
1. Boil the mafaldine until al dente, then drain and set aside, reserving a bit of pasta water.
2. Pat the chicken dry and season it generously. He uses paprika, oregano, thyme, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
3. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat and sear the chicken about 5 minutes per side, until golden.
4. Lower the heat, cover, and cook another 10–12 minutes, until the chicken reaches 165°F. Remove it and let it rest.
5. In the same pan, melt the butter, then add the shallots, garlic powder, and Italian seasoning. Stir about 1 minute to release the flavor.
6. Add the flour and stir until a paste forms, scraping up the browned bits. Slowly whisk in the chicken broth and heavy cream until the sauce is smooth and creamy.
7. Stir in the Parmesan until it’s fully melted and silky. If you’re using roasted garlic, mash it and whisk it into the sauce now.
8. Toss in the pasta, adding a little reserved pasta water at a time until the sauce coats evenly.
9. Slice the chicken, add it back to the pan, stir, and serve hot.

## Tips
- He calls this a one-pan chicken pasta, mostly because he hates doing dishes. On the written recipe the pasta is boiled separately, then tossed in the sauce pan with the chicken.
- Mafaldine (also called mafalda or riccioline) is a ruffled ribbon pasta. Fettuccine or tagliatelle would be the closest swap if you can’t find it *(swap is a suggestion, not from the recipe)*.
- 12 oz of Parmesan is a very generous amount as written on his page. Use what the recipe says, and add the reserved pasta water so the sauce stays silky.
- The roasted garlic is optional.
