# One Pot Creamy Cajun Turkey Sausage and Shrimp Pasta (DASH)

**Based on:** [[One Pot Creamy Cajun Sausage and Shrimp Pasta]]
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 10 min · **Cook:** 25–30 min
**Pan:** a large deep skillet

> DASH swaps: half a pound of lean turkey seasoned like andouille instead of a pound of sausage, a salt-free Cajun blend, no-salt-added fire-roasted tomatoes, low-sodium broth, evaporated milk and light cream cheese instead of heavy cream, whole-wheat rigatoni, less Parmesan, and zucchini.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil, divided
- ½ lb lean ground turkey (93% lean)
- ½ tsp fennel seed, lightly crushed
- ½ lb shrimp, peeled and deveined
- ½ large onion, sliced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 1 zucchini, diced
- 1 can (14.5 oz) no-salt-added fire-roasted diced tomatoes
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2½ cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 8 oz whole wheat rigatoni
- ½ cup fat-free evaporated milk
- 2 oz light cream cheese
- ¼ cup shredded Parmesan
- Fresh parsley, chopped

**Salt-free Cajun blend (about 2 tbsp)**
- 2 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ¼ tsp cayenne

## Steps
1. Mix the Cajun blend. Heat ½ tbsp oil in the skillet over medium-high. Brown the turkey with the fennel and a third of the blend, 4–5 min. Move to a plate.
2. Toss the shrimp with another third of the blend and sear 1–2 min a side until just pink. Move to the plate.
3. Add the rest of the oil, the onion, pepper, and zucchini. Cook until they start to soften, 3–4 min.
4. Stir in the tomatoes, garlic, and the rest of the blend; cook 1 min.
5. Add the broth and rigatoni. Simmer, stirring often, until the pasta is tender and the sauce thick, 12–15 min. Add a splash of water if it dries out.
6. Stir in the evaporated milk and cream cheese until creamy, then the Parmesan.
7. Add the turkey and shrimp back for a minute to heat through. Finish with parsley.
