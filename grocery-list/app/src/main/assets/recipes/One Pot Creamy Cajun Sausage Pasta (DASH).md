# One Pot Creamy Cajun Turkey Sausage Pasta (DASH)

**Based on:** [[One Pot Creamy Cajun Sausage Pasta]]
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 10 min · **Cook:** 25–30 min
**Pan:** a deep 10–12" straight-sided skillet

> DASH swaps: lean ground turkey seasoned like sausage (fennel, smoked paprika, Cajun spices) instead of smoked sausage, a salt-free Cajun blend, no-salt-added tomatoes, low-sodium chicken broth instead of beef broth, evaporated milk and a little light cream cheese instead of heavy cream, whole-wheat rigatoni, less cheese, and spinach.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 lb lean ground turkey (93% lean)
- 1 tsp fennel seed, lightly crushed
- 1 medium white onion, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1 can (14.5 oz) no-salt-added diced tomatoes
- 2½ cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 8 oz whole wheat rigatoni
- ½ cup fat-free evaporated milk
- 2 oz light cream cheese
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan
- 3 cups baby spinach
- ¼ cup shredded part-skim mozzarella
- Chopped fresh parsley

**Salt-free Cajun blend (about 2 tbsp)**
- 2 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ¼ tsp cayenne

## Steps
1. Mix the Cajun blend. Heat ½ tbsp oil in the skillet over medium-high. Brown the turkey with the fennel and half the blend, breaking it into chunks, 5–6 min. Move to a bowl.
2. Add the rest of the oil, the onion, and pepper. Cook until soft, 3–4 min.
3. Stir in the rest of the blend and the garlic; cook 30 seconds.
4. Add the tomatoes with their juice and simmer a few minutes.
5. Pour in the broth and add the rigatoni. Simmer, stirring often, until the pasta is al dente and most of the liquid is absorbed, 12–15 min. Add a splash of water if it dries out.
6. Lower the heat. Stir in the evaporated milk, cream cheese, and Parmesan until smooth.
7. Stir the turkey back in with the spinach. Taste and add more cayenne if you want it hotter.
8. Sprinkle the mozzarella on top, cover 1–2 min to melt, and garnish with parsley.
