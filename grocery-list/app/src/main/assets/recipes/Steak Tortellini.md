# Steak Tortellini

**Source:** [Facebook reel](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1557300292751009/) (shared via https://www.facebook.com/share/r/19voadaSoZ/). Full recipe: [Bad Batch Baking](https://badbatchbaking.com/steak-tortellini/)
**Creator:** Mallory Austin, Bad Batch Baking
**Servings:** 4
**Prep time:** 10 min
**Cook time:** 30 min (40 min total)

> The reel is a 16-second montage set to music ("At Last"), so all amounts, times, and steps come from Mallory's written recipe on her site. The reel caption also says store-bought ravioli can be swapped for the tortellini.

## Ingredients

**For the steak**
- 1½ lbs steak (sirloin, ribeye, or filet), cut into bite-sized pieces
- 3 tbsp avocado oil, divided
- 2 tbsp Cajun seasoning

**For the pasta and sauce**
- 18 oz cheese tortellini
- 4 tbsp butter, divided
- 5 cloves garlic, minced, divided
- 1 cup beef stock
- 1 tsp Cajun seasoning
- cracked black pepper, to taste
- 1½ cups heavy cream
- 2 oz Velveeta, cubed
- 1 cup Parmesan cheese

## Steps
1. Toss the steak evenly with 1 tbsp avocado oil and 2 tbsp Cajun seasoning.
2. Heat a large pan over medium heat and add the remaining 2 tbsp avocado oil *(the recipe lists 3 tbsp oil, divided, and uses 1 tbsp on the steak; the written method says to add avocado oil to the pan)*.
3. Add the steak bites and cook undisturbed for 2 minutes to develop a crust. Flip and cook another 2 minutes.
4. Reduce the heat to low, add 2 tbsp butter and 2 cloves garlic, and toss. Cook 1–2 minutes more.
5. Move the steak to a plate and tent it with foil to keep warm.
6. In the same pan over medium-low heat, add the remaining 2 tbsp butter and 3 cloves garlic. Cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
7. Pour in the beef stock and raise the heat to medium. Simmer 5–7 minutes, until slightly reduced.
8. Meanwhile, cook the tortellini according to the package directions. Drain.
9. Stir 1 tsp Cajun seasoning and cracked black pepper into the sauce.
10. Reduce the heat to low and stir in the heavy cream.
11. Add the cubed Velveeta and whisk until it's fully melted and smooth.
12. Simmer about 5 minutes, until slightly thickened, then stir in the Parmesan until melted.
13. Add the tortellini and stir to coat. Bring to a low boil for 1–2 minutes to thicken.
14. Take the pan off the heat, top with the garlic butter steak bites, and serve immediately.

## Tips
- Let the steak sear undisturbed so it gets a golden crust.
- Use a high-smoke-point oil; Mallory says avocado oil works best for steak bites.
- Lower the heat before adding butter and garlic so the garlic doesn't scorch and turn bitter.
- Cut the Velveeta into small cubes so it melts faster and more smoothly.
- Don't overcook the steak; it keeps cooking a little after you take it out. Her timing guide (plus 1–2 minutes in the butter, except as noted): rare, 2 minutes per side; medium-rare, 2–3 minutes per side; medium, 3 minutes per side; medium-well, 3–4 minutes per side; well done, 4+ minutes per side.
- Don't overcrowd the pan; cook the steak in batches if you need to.
- Store leftovers in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat gently with a splash of cream or broth to loosen the sauce.
