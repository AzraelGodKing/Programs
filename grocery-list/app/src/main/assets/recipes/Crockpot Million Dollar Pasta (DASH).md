# Crockpot Million Dollar Pasta (DASH)

**Based on:** [[Crockpot Million Dollar Pasta]]
**Servings:** 8

> DASH swaps: no-salt-added crushed tomatoes instead of jarred marinara, part-skim ricotta and light cream cheese instead of jarred Alfredo and a full block, no added salt, a homemade salt-free seasoning instead of the store blend, whole-wheat rotini, and zucchini and spinach.

## Ingredients
- 2 lb boneless skinless chicken breast
- 1 can (28 oz) no-salt-added crushed tomatoes
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- ½ tsp red pepper flakes
- 2 zucchini, diced
- 4 oz light cream cheese
- ½ cup part-skim ricotta
- 16 oz whole wheat rotini
- 4 cups baby spinach
- ½ cup shredded part-skim mozzarella
- Parsley flakes, for garnish

## Directions
1. Add the chicken to the crockpot and season it with the pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, smoked paprika, Italian seasoning, and red pepper flakes.
2. Pour in the crushed tomatoes and add the garlic and zucchini.
3. Cover and cook on HIGH about 3 hours, until the chicken reaches 165°F. (With 3 cups of shredded rotisserie chicken instead, cook on LOW 2–3 hours, but note store rotisserie chicken is brined and much saltier.)
4. Shred the chicken and return it. Stir in the cream cheese and ricotta until creamy.
5. Boil the rotini in unsalted water, drain, and stir it in with the spinach and mozzarella.
6. Finish with parsley flakes.
