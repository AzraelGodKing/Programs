# Creamy Chicken and Shrimp Fettuccine Alfredo (DASH)

**Based on:** [[Creamy Chicken and Shrimp Fettuccine Alfredo]]
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 15 min · **Cook:** 25 min

> DASH swaps: a light Alfredo made from 1% milk thickened with flour instead of heavy cream and butter, a homemade salt-free blackening blend, whole-wheat fettuccine, less Parmesan, and broccoli tossed in.

## Ingredients
- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breast
- ½ lb shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 8 oz whole wheat fettuccine
- 3 cups broccoli florets
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 2 cups 1% milk
- ⅓ cup grated Parmesan
- ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
- 1 lemon
- Fresh parsley, chopped

**Salt-free blackening blend**
- 2 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ¼ tsp cayenne

## Steps
1. Boil the fettuccine in unsalted water. Add the broccoli for the last 3 minutes. Save ½ cup of pasta water, then drain.
2. Mix the blend. Rub the chicken with 1 tbsp olive oil and most of the blend; toss the shrimp with the rest.
3. Sear the chicken in a large pan over medium-high until blackened and cooked through to 165°F, 5–6 min a side. Set aside. Sear the shrimp 1–2 min a side and set aside.
4. Lower the heat to medium. Add 1 tbsp olive oil and the garlic and cook until fragrant, 30 seconds.
5. Whisk in the flour and cook 1 min, then slowly whisk in the milk. Simmer gently, stirring, until it thickens, 3–4 min.
6. Off the heat, stir in the Parmesan, red pepper flakes, and a squeeze of lemon.
7. Toss in the pasta and broccoli, loosening with pasta water as needed. Slice the chicken and serve with the shrimp on top, parsley, and lemon wedges.

## Tips
- Shrimp are quick: pull them as soon as they turn pink and curl.
- This sauce tightens as it cools; a splash of pasta water fixes it.
