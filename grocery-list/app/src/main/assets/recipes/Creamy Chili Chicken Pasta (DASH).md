# Creamy Chili Chicken Pasta (DASH)

**Based on:** [[Creamy Chili Chicken Pasta]]
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 15 min · **Cook:** 25 min

> DASH swaps: 1% milk instead of whole milk and heavy cream, olive oil instead of butter, no bouillon or added salt, part-skim mozzarella and a little Parmesan instead of three cheeses, whole-wheat shells, and red pepper and spinach added. The Calabrian chili paste stays; it's where the flavor is.

## Ingredients

**For the chicken**
- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breast, cut into small cubes
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- ½ tsp Italian seasoning
- ½ tsp chili powder
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp olive oil

**For the pasta and sauce**
- 10 oz whole wheat shell pasta
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 2 cups 1% milk
- 1 tbsp Calabrian chili paste
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ½ cup shredded part-skim mozzarella
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan
- 3 cups baby spinach

## Steps
1. Toss the chicken with the spices and lemon juice. Cook in 1 tbsp olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high until golden and cooked through. Set aside.
2. Boil the shells in unsalted water until al dente. Save ½ cup pasta water and drain.
3. In the same skillet, heat 1 tbsp olive oil over medium. Cook the bell pepper 3 min, then the garlic 30 seconds.
4. Whisk in the flour and cook 1 min. Slowly whisk in the milk and simmer gently until slightly thick, 3 min.
5. Stir in the chili paste and garlic powder. Lower the heat and stir in the mozzarella and Parmesan until smooth.
6. Add the spinach, pasta, and chicken and toss, loosening with pasta water a tablespoon at a time.
7. Finish with black pepper and a little extra chili paste if you like it hotter.

## Tips
- Taste before adding any salt; the chili paste and cheese bring plenty.
