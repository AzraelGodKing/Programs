# Crockpot Garlic Parmesan Chicken Pasta (DASH)

**Based on:** [[Crockpot Garlic Parmesan Chicken Pasta]]
**Servings:** 6
**Prep:** 10 min · **Cook:** 3 hours on HIGH

> DASH swaps: a from-scratch garlic sauce (low-sodium broth, lots of garlic, milk) instead of jarred garlic Parmesan sauce, half the cream cheese and the light kind, whole-wheat pasta, and broccoli.

## Ingredients
- 1½ lb boneless skinless chicken breast
- 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 4 oz light cream cheese
- 1 cup 1% milk
- 2 tbsp cornstarch
- ½ cup grated Parmesan
- 12 oz whole wheat pasta
- 3 cups broccoli florets
- 1 lemon
- Fresh parsley, chopped

## Steps
1. Put the chicken, broth, garlic, Italian seasoning, onion powder, and pepper in the crockpot. Cook on HIGH 3 hours.
2. Take the chicken out and shred it.
3. Whisk the cornstarch into the milk, then stir it and the cream cheese into the crockpot. Cover 10–15 min until thick, then stir in the Parmesan.
4. Meanwhile, boil the pasta in unsalted water, adding the broccoli for the last 3 minutes. Drain.
5. Stir the chicken, pasta, and broccoli into the sauce. Finish with lemon juice, cracked pepper, and parsley.
