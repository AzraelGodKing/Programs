# Crockpot Cheesy Marinara Chicken

**Source:** [Facebook reel by Rileys Stupid Recipes](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1489068496405726/) (shared as https://www.facebook.com/share/r/1WFfA33pHu/), "Crockpot Chronicles Episode 3"
**Servings:** 12
**Prep:** ~15 min *(estimate)* · **Cook:** 5 hr on HIGH or 8 hr on LOW
**Per serving (creator's caption):** 394 cal · 48 g protein · 7 g carbs · 18 g fat
**Whole pot:** 4,730 cal · 570 g protein · 88 g carbs · 220 g fat

## Ingredients
- 4 lb boneless skinless chicken breast (or tenderloins)
- 20 oz marinara sauce (1 jar)
- 16 oz Alfredo sauce (1 jar)
- 1 yellow onion, finely diced
- ¼ cup diced cherry peppers (optional)
- 1 tbsp ranch powder
- 1 tbsp Italian seasoning, plus a pinch for the top
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 8 oz freshly shredded mozzarella

### Optional base (how he served it)
- About 2 lb sweet potatoes, diced *(amount estimated)*
- 1–2 tbsp olive oil, plus salt, pepper, and herbs *(estimate)*

## Steps
1. Put the chicken in a large slow cooker (6 qt or bigger *(estimate)*). Add the marinara, Alfredo, onion, cherry peppers, ranch powder, Italian seasoning, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Mix well.
2. Cover and cook on HIGH for 5 hours or LOW for 8 hours, until the chicken is tender and falls apart.
3. Shred the chicken right in the pot and stir it into the sauce.
4. Top with the mozzarella and a sprinkle of Italian seasoning. Put the lid back on until the cheese melts, about 10 min *(estimate)*.
5. Serve plain, over rice, over potatoes, or with garlic bread.

### Optional sweet potato base
6. Toss the diced sweet potatoes with oil and seasoning. Roast at 425°F for 25–30 min or air-fry at 400°F for 15–20 min, shaking halfway, until browned at the edges *(times and temps estimated)*. Ladle the chicken over the top.

## Tips
- Cherry peppers add a little heat and tang. Leave them out if you want it milder.
- Shredding it yourself from a block of mozzarella melts better than pre-shredded.
- It portions into 12 meal-prep servings and keeps about 4 days in the fridge *(estimate, standard food safety)*.
