# High-Protein Creamy Parmesan Chicken Pasta

**Source:** [Facebook reel by Samuel Benjamin](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1085459764252102/) (shared as https://www.facebook.com/share/r/1G3oM37CV7/)
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** ~15 min *(estimate)* · **Cook:** ~20 min *(estimate)*
**Per serving (creator's caption):** ~590 cal · 60 g protein · 32 g carbs · 25 g fat (he says 530 cal out loud in the video)

## Ingredients
- 1¼ lb boneless skinless chicken breast, diced bite-size
- 8 oz penne pasta
- ¾ cup Olive Garden Light Italian Dressing
- ½ medium white onion, diced
- 8 oz reduced-fat cream cheese
- ½ cup roasted red bell peppers, drained and diced
- 1 cup shredded Parmesan
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- ½ tsp black pepper
- Nonstick cooking spray
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- Salt for the pasta water *(estimate)*

## Steps
1. Boil the penne in salted water until al dente (about 10–11 min, check the box *(estimate)*). Drain and set aside.
2. Toss the diced chicken with the garlic powder, onion powder, Italian seasoning, and black pepper.
3. Spray a large skillet (he uses an electric skillet) and heat over medium-high. Cook the chicken, stirring, until browned and cooked through to 165°F, about 6–8 min *(estimate)*.
4. Pour in the Italian dressing, then add the onion. Simmer a few minutes until the onion softens *(time estimated)*.
5. Add the cream cheese and stir until it melts into a smooth, creamy sauce. Lower the heat to medium-low.
6. Stir in the cooked penne and roasted red peppers, then the Parmesan. Mix until the cheese melts and everything's coated.
7. Divide into 4 servings and top with parsley.

## Tips
- In the video he uses regular Olive Garden Signature Italian and full-fat Philadelphia, and pours well over a cup of dressing. The written recipe above (light dressing, reduced-fat cream cheese, ¾ cup) is what gets you his macros.
- If the sauce gets too thick, loosen it with a splash of pasta water or milk.
- It keeps in the fridge about 4 days *(estimate, standard food safety)* for meal prep.
