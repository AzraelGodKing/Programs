# Crockpot Creamy Meatball Pasta (DASH)

**Based on:** [[Crockpot Creamy Meatball Pasta]]
**Servings:** 6
**Prep:** 25 min · **Cook:** 2½ hours on HIGH

> DASH swaps: quick homemade lean turkey meatballs instead of frozen beef/pork ones (which are salty), no-salt-added crushed tomatoes instead of jarred marinara, light cream cheese instead of Alfredo plus a full block, herbs instead of garlic salt and seasoning blends, whole-wheat bowties, half the mozzarella, and spinach.

## Ingredients

**Turkey meatballs**
- 1¼ lb lean ground turkey (93% lean)
- ½ cup whole wheat panko
- 1 egg
- 2 tbsp grated Parmesan
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp black pepper

**Sauce and pasta**
- 1 can (28 oz) no-salt-added crushed tomatoes
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
- 4 oz light cream cheese
- 12 oz whole wheat bowtie pasta
- 1 cup shredded part-skim mozzarella
- 3 cups baby spinach
- Parsley flakes, for topping

## Steps
1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Mix the meatball ingredients, roll into about 24 balls, and bake on a lined pan 15 min. (They finish cooking in the sauce.)
2. Put the meatballs in the crockpot with the tomatoes, garlic, and seasonings. Add the cream cheese on top.
3. Cook on HIGH 2½ hours.
4. Boil the bowties in unsalted water and drain.
5. Stir the cream cheese into the sauce, then add the pasta, spinach, and mozzarella. Mix until melted and creamy.
6. Top with parsley.

## Tips
- Make a double batch of meatballs and freeze half; they go straight from the freezer into the crockpot next time.
