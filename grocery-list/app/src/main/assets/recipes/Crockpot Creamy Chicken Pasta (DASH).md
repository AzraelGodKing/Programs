# Crockpot Creamy Chicken Pasta (DASH)

**Based on:** [[Crockpot Creamy Chicken Pasta]]
**Servings:** 8
**Prep:** 15 min · **Cook:** 4–6 hours on LOW (or 3–4 on HIGH)

> DASH swaps: no-salt-added crushed tomatoes with herbs instead of jarred marinara, light cream cheese and milk instead of jarred Alfredo, no added salt, whole-wheat pasta, a bit more chicken, and spinach stirred in.

## Ingredients
- 1½ lb boneless skinless chicken breast
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 2 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1 tsp dried basil
- ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 can (28 oz) no-salt-added crushed tomatoes
- 4 oz light cream cheese
- ½ cup 1% milk
- 1 cup shredded part-skim mozzarella
- 16 oz whole wheat rotini (or cellentani)
- 4 cups fresh spinach
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan
- Dried parsley
- Cooking spray

## Steps
1. Spray the crockpot. Add the chicken, season it with the pepper, onion powder, Italian seasoning, basil, and red pepper flakes, then add the garlic and crushed tomatoes.
2. Cook on LOW 4–6 hours (HIGH 3–4) until the chicken reaches 165°F.
3. Take the chicken out and cube or shred it. Stir the cream cheese and milk into the sauce until smooth, then add the chicken back with the mozzarella. Keep on LOW.
4. Boil the pasta in unsalted water, drain, and stir it in with the spinach until wilted.
5. Top with Parmesan and parsley.

## Tips
- Serve with roasted broccoli or green beans and a side salad.
- Reheat leftovers on the stove with a splash of milk.
