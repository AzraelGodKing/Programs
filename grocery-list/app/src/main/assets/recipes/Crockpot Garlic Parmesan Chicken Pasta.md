# Crockpot Garlic Parmesan Chicken Pasta

**Source:** [Facebook reel](https://www.facebook.com/reel/4596524937248952/) (shared via https://www.facebook.com/share/r/1CKps9WUH8/)
**Creator:** Rian Meck Cooks
**Servings:** 6 *(estimate)*
**Prep time:** 5–10 min *(estimate)* to load the crockpot, plus time to boil the pasta later
**Cook time:** 3 hours on HIGH, plus the pasta cooked according to the box

## Ingredients
- 3 chicken breasts (1½–2 lb)
- 1 (8 oz) block cream cheese
- ½ cup Parmesan cheese
- 12 oz garlic parmesan sauce
- 1 lb box pasta of your choice
- black pepper, for topping
- parsley, for topping

## Steps
1. Add the chicken, cream cheese, Parmesan, and garlic parmesan sauce to the crockpot. Cook on HIGH for 3 hours.
2. When the chicken is done, take each piece out and shred it into small pieces with two forks. Add the chicken back to the crockpot and stir everything together.
3. Boil the pasta according to the box directions, then drain it. Add it to the crockpot and stir until the pasta is fully coated.
4. Serve in a bowl and top with fresh cracked black pepper and chopped parsley.

## Tips
- Any pasta shape you like works; the caption just says a 1 lb box of your choice.
- No LOW cook time is given, only HIGH for 3 hours.
- The caption doesn't name a brand for the 12 oz garlic parmesan sauce.
