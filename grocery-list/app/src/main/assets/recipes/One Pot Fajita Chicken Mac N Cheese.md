# One Pot Fajita Chicken Mac N Cheese

**Source:** [Facebook reel](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1093786976728401/) (shared via https://www.facebook.com/share/r/1GRupiXEQg/). He says the full recipe is in his bio. His recipe hub is [Provecho — zachs.foods](https://www.provecho.co/zachs.foods), and a matching listing is [One Pot Fajita Chicken Mac N Cheese](https://www.provecho.co/platform/recipe/EwyWv93GYgYKafVovdvT). The public page was truncated, so the amounts below come from the Facebook caption and the steps come from the spoken reel.
**Creator:** Zachs.foods ("Recipes that are better than takeout," Ep. 48)
**Servings:** 4 *(listed on his Provecho recipe page)*
**Prep time:** 10 min *(estimate)*
**Cook time:** about 15 min to simmer the pasta, plus time to sear the chicken and soften the vegetables; about 25–30 min total *(total is an estimate)*

## Ingredients

**For the chicken**
- 2 large chicken breasts
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp salt
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp chili powder
- ½ tsp oregano
- ½ tsp cumin
- juice of ½ lime *(estimate; he says "a nice squeeze of lime juice," no amount given)*

**Vegetables and pasta**
- 1 bell pepper
- ½ red onion
- 2 garlic cloves
- 8–10 oz uncooked shell pasta
- a pinch of salt
- about 2 cups water, just enough to cover the pasta

**For the cheese sauce (second spice set)**
- 12 oz evaporated milk
- 4 oz cheddar
- 4 oz mozzarella
- 4 oz pepper jack
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp chili powder
- ½ tsp oregano
- ½ tsp cumin
- 1 tbsp cilantro

## Steps
1. Season the chicken with the olive oil and the first set of spices (2 tsp salt, 2 tsp garlic powder, 2 tsp onion powder, 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp chili powder, ½ tsp oregano, and ½ tsp cumin). Sear until cooked through, then take it out of the pan.
2. Give the cooked chicken a nice squeeze of lime juice (about ½ lime).
3. Add the onion and bell pepper to the same pan. Once they're soft, add the garlic.
4. Add a pinch of salt, the dry shell pasta, and just enough water to cover (about 2 cups). Simmer about 15 minutes, until most of the water has cooked off.
5. Stir in the evaporated milk, the cheddar, mozzarella, and pepper jack, and the second spice set (1 tsp salt, 1 tsp garlic powder, 1 tsp onion powder, 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp chili powder, ½ tsp oregano, and ½ tsp cumin). Mix until the sauce is nice and creamy.
6. Add the chicken back in.
7. Top with the chopped cilantro and serve.

## Tips
