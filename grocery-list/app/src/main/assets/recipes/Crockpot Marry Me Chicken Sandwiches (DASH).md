# Crockpot Marry Me Chicken Sandwiches (DASH)

**Based on:** [[Crockpot Marry Me Chicken Sandwiches]]
**Servings:** 6 sandwiches
**Prep:** 10 min · **Cook:** 4–6 hr on LOW

> DASH swaps: no bacon or buttered brioche (whole-wheat buns toasted dry, with spinach and tomato instead), half the cream cheese and the light kind, part-skim mozzarella, low-sodium broth, and a salt-free seasoning mix.

## Ingredients
- 1½ lb boneless skinless chicken breast
- 1 tbsp Italian seasoning
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
- ⅓ cup oil-packed sun-dried tomatoes, drained and chopped
- ¾ cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 4 oz light cream cheese
- ½ cup shredded part-skim mozzarella
- 6 whole wheat buns, toasted
- 2 cups baby spinach
- 2 tomatoes, sliced

## Steps
1. Put the chicken in a 6 qt slow cooker. Add the seasonings, garlic, red pepper flakes, sun-dried tomatoes, and broth.
2. Cover and cook on LOW 4–6 hours, until the chicken is 165°F and tender.
3. Shred the chicken in the pot (a hand mixer on low is fastest). Spoon off extra liquid if it looks soupy.
4. Stir in the cream cheese and mozzarella, cover, and let it melt 10 min. Stir until creamy.
5. Toast the buns in a dry skillet or the toaster.
6. Pile on the chicken, top with spinach and tomato slices, and close.

## Tips
- Serve with roasted broccoli or a salad instead of tater tots.
- The filling is also good over brown rice or in a whole wheat wrap.
