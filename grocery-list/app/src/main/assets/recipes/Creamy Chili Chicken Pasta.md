# Creamy Chili Chicken Pasta

**Source:** [Facebook reel](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1047412564931873/) (shared via https://www.facebook.com/share/r/1EEqnqoyUT/)
**Creator:** Im’s foodstory
**Servings:** 4 *(estimate)*
**Prep time:** 15 min *(estimate)*
**Cook time:** 20–25 min *(estimate)*

> The written recipe is in the reel caption. Whisper did not recover a usable spoken recipe from the audio.

## Ingredients

**For the chicken**
- 1 lb boneless chicken breast, cut into small cubes
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp paprika powder
- ½ tsp Italian seasoning
- ½ tsp chili powder
- ½ tsp onion powder *(estimate; used in the method, no amount given)*
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- salt, to taste
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp olive oil

**For the pasta**
- 12 oz medium shell pasta
- salt, for the pasta water

**For the creamy sauce**
- 3 tbsp butter
- 5 cloves fresh garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1 cup whole milk
- 1 cup heavy whipping cream
- 1½ tbsp Calabrian chili paste
- 1 tsp chicken bouillon powder
- ½ cup freshly grated Parmesan
- ½ cup mozzarella cheese
- ½ cup cheddar cheese
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- salt and pepper, to taste
- ¼ cup reserved pasta water (the method also says to reserve about ½ cup, then add it a tablespoon at a time)

**For serving**
- extra freshly grated Parmesan
- a drizzle of Calabrian chili oil
- freshly cracked black pepper

## Steps
1. In a bowl, season the chicken with the garlic powder, paprika, Italian seasoning, chili powder, onion powder, black pepper, lemon juice, and salt.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and cook until golden brown and cooked through. Move it out of the skillet and set it aside.
3. Bring a large pot of generously salted water to a boil. Cook the shell pasta until al dente according to the package directions. Reserve about ½ cup of pasta water, then drain.
4. In the same skillet, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and sauté for about 30–60 seconds, until fragrant.
5. Whisk in the flour and cook for about 1 minute.
6. Slowly whisk in the whole milk and heavy cream until smooth. Let the sauce simmer gently for 2–3 minutes, stirring occasionally, until slightly thickened.
7. Stir in the Calabrian chili paste, chicken bouillon powder, and garlic powder. Season with black pepper, then taste before adding additional salt.
8. Reduce the heat to low, or turn it off. Gradually add the Parmesan, mozzarella, and cheddar, stirring until melted and completely smooth.
9. Add the cooked shell pasta and the chicken to the sauce. Toss until everything is evenly coated. Add reserved pasta water, a tablespoon at a time, until the sauce is silky and creamy.
10. Top with freshly grated Parmesan, a drizzle of Calabrian chili oil, and freshly cracked black pepper. Serve immediately.

## Tips
- The method uses onion powder on the chicken, but onion powder is not in the ingredient list. The ½ tsp above is an estimate; no amount was given.
- Taste the sauce before adding more salt; the bouillon and cheeses already add salt.
- Add the cheeses over low heat or off the heat so the sauce stays smooth.
- The finish calls for a drizzle of Calabrian chili oil, which is separate from the 1½ tbsp of Calabrian chili paste in the sauce. No amount was given for the oil.
