# Creamy Garlic Cheesy Chicken and Potatoes (DASH)

**Based on:** [[Creamy Garlic Cheesy Chicken and Potatoes]]
**Servings:** 8
**Prep:** 25 min · **Cook:** 45–47 min
**Oven:** 200°C / 400°F
**Pan:** two large lined sheet pans (or one very large one)

> DASH swaps: the 3 tsp of salt drops to ½ tsp (that alone cuts about 750 mg a serving), 1% milk, less cream cheese and mozzarella, and a pan of green beans and peppers roasted alongside. Potatoes are a good DASH food; they're high in potassium.

## Ingredients

**Chicken and seasonings**
- 800g boneless skinless chicken breast, cubed
- 800g boneless skinless chicken thighs, trimmed and cubed
- ½ tsp salt
- 5 tsp Italian seasoning
- 4 tsp garlic powder
- 4 tsp onion powder
- 4 tsp paprika
- 2 tbsp olive oil

**Roasted vegetables**
- 1600g potatoes, cubed
- 1 red onion, chopped
- 2 bell peppers, chopped
- 1 lb green beans, trimmed
- 3 tsp Italian seasoning
- 3 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp chili flakes
- 3 tsp paprika
- 1 tbsp olive oil

**Creamy sauce and garnish**
- 1½ cups 1% milk
- 6 oz light garlic and herb cream cheese
- 2 oz shredded part-skim mozzarella
- Fresh parsley, chopped

## Steps
1. Toss the chicken with the salt, Italian seasoning, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, and 2 tbsp olive oil. Set aside.
2. Toss the potatoes, onion, peppers, and green beans with the vegetable spices and 1 tbsp olive oil. Spread over the sheet pans and roast at 200°C / 400°F for 20–22 minutes.
3. Spread the chicken over the vegetables in a single layer and roast another 25 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through.
4. Take the pans out. Add the milk, cream cheese, and mozzarella and stir; the heat melts it into a sauce.
5. Garnish with parsley and divide into 8 containers.

## Tips
- If one pan is crowded, the potatoes steam instead of crisping, so split it across two.
- Keeps 4 days in the fridge.
