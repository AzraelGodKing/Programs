# Crockpot Million Dollar Pasta

Source: Sara Rolinscott's Facebook reel (recipe from the comments).

## Ingredients
- 2 lbs boneless, skinless chicken breasts OR about 3 cups shredded rotisserie chicken
- 1 jar (15 oz) marinara sauce
- 1 jar (15 oz) Alfredo sauce
- 1 block (8 oz) cream cheese
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- Fire & Smoke Society Chicka Licka Bam Bam seasoning, to taste
- Rotini noodles (16 oz)
- Parsley flakes, for garnish

## Directions
### Option 1: Raw chicken
1. Add the chicken breasts to the crockpot and season with salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and Chicka Licka Bam Bam.
2. Pour in the marinara and Alfredo. Add the cream cheese and minced garlic, then gently stir the sauces around the chicken.
3. Cover and cook on HIGH for about 3 hours, until the chicken reaches 165°F and is tender.
4. Remove the chicken, shred it with two forks, and return it to the crockpot.

### Option 2: Rotisserie chicken
1. Add about 3 cups of shredded rotisserie chicken to the crockpot.
2. Add the marinara, Alfredo, cream cheese, minced garlic, and seasonings. Stir to combine.
3. Cover and cook on LOW for 2–3 hours or HIGH for about 1–1½ hours, until heated through and the cream cheese has melted. Stir occasionally.
4. Stir until melted and creamy.

Finish with parsley flakes and serve over the cooked rotini (boil it separately and stir it in at the end).
