# Cowboy Butter Chicken Orzo (DASH)

**Based on:** [[Cowboy Butter Chicken Orzo]]
**Servings:** 6
**Prep:** 10 min · **Cook:** 25–30 min
**Pan:** a large covered skillet or electric skillet

> DASH swaps: a homemade salt-free "cowboy butter" blend instead of the store blends, olive oil for the butter, low-sodium broth, fat-free evaporated milk for the heavy cream, less Parmesan, and spinach and peas folded in. Serve with a salad instead of garlic knots.

## Ingredients
- 1½ lb boneless skinless chicken breast, diced
- 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 2 cups dry orzo (whole wheat if you can find it)
- 3½ cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 2 tbsp lemon juice, plus the zest of 1 lemon
- ½ cup fat-free evaporated milk
- ⅓ cup grated Parmesan
- 3 cups baby spinach
- 1 cup frozen peas
- Dried parsley, for garnish

**Salt-free cowboy butter blend**
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp dried parsley
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- ½ tsp red pepper flakes
- ½ tsp black pepper

## Steps
1. Mix the blend. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in the skillet, add the chicken, and season it with about half the blend. Cook just until it has some color.
2. Add the other 1 tbsp olive oil and the orzo. Toast a couple of minutes if you like.
3. Pour in the broth and stir in the Dijon, lemon juice and zest, and the rest of the blend.
4. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook until the liquid is absorbed and the orzo is tender, about 12–15 min. Stir once or twice so it doesn't stick.
5. Stir in the peas and spinach until the spinach wilts, then the evaporated milk and Parmesan until creamy.
6. Finish with parsley and an extra squeeze of lemon.

## Tips
- Evaporated milk stays creamy without splitting, which regular milk can do in a hot pan.
- If the orzo is still firm when the liquid is gone, add broth a few tablespoons at a time.
