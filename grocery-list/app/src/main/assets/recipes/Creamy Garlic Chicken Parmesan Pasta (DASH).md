# Creamy Garlic Chicken Parmesan Pasta (DASH)

**Based on:** [[Creamy Garlic Chicken Parmesan Pasta]]
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 15 min · **Cook:** 30 min

> DASH swaps: the biggest one is the cheese: ½ cup of Parmesan instead of 12 oz, and 1% milk instead of 3 cups of heavy cream. Olive oil replaces the butter, the salt is gone, and roasted garlic and broccoli carry the flavor.

## Ingredients
- 1¼ lb boneless skinless chicken breast
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 shallot, minced
- 1 head garlic, roasted (or 4 cloves, minced)
- 2 tsp Italian seasoning
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- ½ cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 2 cups 1% milk
- ½ cup grated Parmesan
- 10 oz whole wheat fettuccine (or mafaldine)
- 3 cups broccoli florets

## Steps
1. If roasting the garlic: cut the top off the head, drizzle with a little oil, wrap in foil, and bake at 400°F for 40 min. Do this ahead.
2. Boil the pasta in unsalted water, adding the broccoli for the last 3 minutes. Save ½ cup pasta water and drain.
3. Pat the chicken dry and season with the garlic powder, paprika, oregano, thyme, and pepper.
4. Sear in 1 tbsp olive oil over medium, about 5 min per side, then cover and cook another 10–12 min to 165°F. Rest, then slice.
5. In the same pan, heat 1 tbsp olive oil. Cook the shallot 2 min, then the Italian seasoning 30 seconds.
6. Stir in the flour, then slowly whisk in the broth and milk. Simmer gently until it thickens.
7. Off the heat, stir in the Parmesan and mash in the roasted garlic.
8. Toss in the pasta and broccoli with a little pasta water, top with the chicken, and serve.
