# Crockpot Marry Me Meatballs (DASH)

**Based on:** [[Crockpot Marry Me Meatballs]]
**Servings:** 6
**Prep:** 25 min · **Cook:** 4 hours on LOW

> DASH swaps: homemade lean turkey meatballs bound with oats instead of frozen Italian meatballs, a sauce of low-sodium broth, milk and light cream cheese instead of jarred Alfredo, and spinach stirred in. The creator himself suggests turkey meatballs.

## Ingredients

**Turkey meatballs**
- 1½ lb lean ground turkey (93% lean)
- ½ cup quick oats
- 1 egg
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- ½ tsp onion powder
- ½ tsp black pepper

**Sauce**
- 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 cup 1% milk
- 2 tbsp cornstarch
- 4 oz light cream cheese
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
- ⅓ cup oil-packed sun-dried tomatoes, drained and chopped
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan
- 4 cups baby spinach
- Dried parsley, for topping

**To serve**
- 2 lb potatoes, for mashing with a little milk (or 4 cups cooked brown rice)

## Steps
1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Mix the meatball ingredients, roll into about 24 balls, and bake on a lined pan 15 min.
2. In the crockpot, whisk the cornstarch into the milk, then add the broth, cream cheese, spices, and sun-dried tomatoes. Add the meatballs and stir.
3. Cook on LOW 4 hours, stirring once or twice.
4. Stir in the Parmesan and spinach until wilted. Top with parsley.
5. Serve over potatoes mashed with milk and pepper (no butter or salt needed with this sauce), or brown rice, with green beans on the side.

## Tips
- LOW is better than HIGH here; milk sauces can separate if the crockpot runs hot. A splash of milk fixes a split sauce.
