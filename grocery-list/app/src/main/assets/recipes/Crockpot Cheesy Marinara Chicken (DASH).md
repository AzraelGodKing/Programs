# Crockpot Cheesy Marinara Chicken (DASH)

**Based on:** [[Crockpot Cheesy Marinara Chicken]]
**Servings:** 12
**Prep:** 15 min · **Cook:** 5 hr on HIGH or 8 hr on LOW

> DASH swaps: no-salt-added crushed tomatoes and herbs instead of jarred marinara, Greek yogurt and a little Parmesan stirred in at the end instead of jarred Alfredo, dill and onion powder in place of the ranch packet, part-skim mozzarella, and peppers and mushrooms cooked in. The sweet potato base is a great DASH side; keep it.

## Ingredients
- 4 lb boneless skinless chicken breast (or tenderloins)
- 1 can (28 oz) no-salt-added crushed tomatoes
- 1 yellow onion, finely diced
- 2 bell peppers, diced
- 8 oz mushrooms, sliced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp Italian seasoning, plus a pinch for the top
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp dried dill
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp red pepper flakes
- ½ cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
- ¼ cup grated Parmesan
- 6 oz shredded part-skim mozzarella

**Sweet potato base**
- 3 lb sweet potatoes, diced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Black pepper and herbs

## Steps
1. Put the chicken in a 6 qt or larger slow cooker. Add the tomatoes, onion, peppers, mushrooms, garlic, and seasonings. Mix.
2. Cover and cook on HIGH 5 hours or LOW 8 hours, until the chicken falls apart.
3. Shred the chicken in the pot. Turn the cooker off and stir in the Greek yogurt and Parmesan.
4. Top with the mozzarella and a pinch of Italian seasoning, cover, and let it melt, about 10 min.
5. For the base, toss the sweet potatoes with oil and seasoning and roast at 425°F for 25–30 min (or air-fry at 400°F for 15–20 min). Ladle the chicken over the top, or serve over brown rice.

## Tips
- Want the cherry-pepper tang from the original? Add a diced fresh jalapeño and a splash of red wine vinegar instead; jarred cherry peppers are salty.
- Portions into 12 meal-prep servings; keeps about 4 days.
