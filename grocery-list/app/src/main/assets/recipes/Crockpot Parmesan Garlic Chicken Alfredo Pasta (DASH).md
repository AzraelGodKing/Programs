# Crockpot Parmesan Garlic Chicken Alfredo Pasta (DASH)

**Based on:** [[Crockpot Parmesan Garlic Chicken Alfredo Pasta]]
**Servings:** 6
**Prep:** 15 min · **Cook:** about 6 hr on LOW, plus 15 min to finish

> DASH swaps: a homemade light Alfredo (milk, light cream cheese, Parmesan, lots of garlic) instead of jarred Alfredo and wing sauce, low-sodium broth, a salt-free seasoning mix, whole-wheat cavatappi, less mozzarella, and broccoli.

## Ingredients
- 1½ lb boneless skinless chicken breast
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 2 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 1½ cups 1% milk
- 2 tbsp cornstarch
- 4 oz light cream cheese
- ⅓ cup grated Parmesan
- 12 oz whole wheat cavatappi
- 3 cups broccoli florets
- ¾ cup shredded part-skim mozzarella
- Fresh parsley, chopped

## Steps
1. Put the chicken in a 6 qt slow cooker. Sprinkle on the seasonings, add the garlic and broth.
2. Cover and cook on LOW about 6 hours, until the chicken is 165°F.
3. Take out the chicken and chop it. Whisk the cornstarch into the milk and stir it into the pot with the cream cheese. Cover on HIGH 10–15 min until thick, then stir in the Parmesan.
4. Meanwhile boil the cavatappi in unsalted water, adding the broccoli for the last 3 minutes. Drain.
5. Stir in the chicken, pasta, broccoli, and mozzarella. Top with parsley.

## Tips
- Serve with a side salad with oil and vinegar instead of garlic bread.
- If it looks dry, add a splash of milk.
