# Crockpot Creamy Chicken Pasta

**Source:** [Facebook reel](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1376474707893485/) (shared via https://www.facebook.com/share/r/1DjnYxDdPF/). Full recipe: [Crockpot Creamy Chicken Pasta — Cooking in the Midwest](https://cookinginthemidwest.com/blog/crockpot-creamy-chicken-pasta/)
**Creator:** Cooking in the Midwest (Luke Brown)
**Servings:** 8 (from the blog)
**Prep time:** 15 min (from the blog)
**Cook time:** 4–6 hours on low (the blog lists 6 hours; the post also says you can cook it on high for 3–4 hours if you’re short on time)

The Facebook caption doesn’t include the recipe, but the spoken reel matches this blog post: 1 lb chicken, seasonings, marinara, a jar of Alfredo, cooked on low, then chopped and stirred back in with “squiggly noodles” and cheese, topped with more cheese and parsley. Amounts below are from the blog. Nothing is estimated.

## Ingredients
- 1 lb chicken breast
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp pepper
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- 24 oz jar marinara sauce
- 15 oz jar Alfredo sauce
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
- 16 oz pasta (he likes Cellentani, the “squiggly noodles” from the reel; penne or rigatoni also work)
- ¼ cup Parmesan cheese
- Dried parsley
- Cooking spray, for the crockpot

## Steps
1. Spray a crockpot with cooking spray. Add the chicken breasts and season with the salt, pepper, onion powder, red pepper flakes, and Italian seasoning. Add the minced garlic, marinara, and Alfredo.
2. Cook on low for 4–6 hours, until the chicken is fully cooked and reaches at least 165°F. (On high, about 3–4 hours.)
3. Take the chicken out and shred it, or cube it with a knife (he prefers cubing). Add it back to the crockpot and stir in the mozzarella. Keep it on low while you boil the pasta.
4. Boil the pasta according to the box directions. Drain it, add it to the crockpot, and stir well.
5. Top with the Parmesan and dried parsley, and serve.

## Tips
- He likes Bertolli for the Alfredo and has been using Aldi’s marinara. Use any brands your family likes.
- Shred the mozzarella and Parmesan from blocks for a creamier sauce.
- Fresh or jarred minced garlic both work.
- A big handful of fresh spinach can go in with the mozzarella if you want a vegetable in the pot.
- He serves it with breadsticks or garlic bread and a Caesar salad, or with roasted broccoli or green beans.
- Leftovers keep in the fridge and reheat in the microwave. For best results, reheat on the stove over medium heat, adding a little milk at a time until it’s hot.
- Don’t start with frozen chicken in the crockpot.
