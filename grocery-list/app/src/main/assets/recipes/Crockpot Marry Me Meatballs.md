# Crockpot Marry Me Meatballs

**Source:** [Facebook reel](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1412397000952098/) (shared via https://www.facebook.com/share/r/1ADx23macc/). Full recipe: [Crockpot Marry Me Meatballs — Cooking in the Midwest](https://cookinginthemidwest.com/blog/crockpot-marry-me-meatballs/)
**Creator:** Cooking in the Midwest (Luke Brown)
**Servings:** 6 (from the blog)
**Prep time:** 5 min (from the blog)
**Cook time:** 4 hours on low (from the blog; he says this timing is best in his crockpot)

The Facebook caption doesn’t include the recipe, but the spoken reel matches this blog post: frozen meatballs, Alfredo, milk, seasoning, sun-dried tomatoes, and Parmesan, cooked on low about 4 hours, then served over mashed potatoes. Amounts below are from the blog. Nothing is estimated.

## Ingredients
- 1½ lbs frozen Italian-style meatballs, store-bought or homemade
- 15 oz jar Alfredo sauce
- ½ tsp pepper
- ½ tsp oregano
- ½ tsp thyme
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ¼ tsp red pepper flakes
- ⅓ cup chopped sun-dried tomatoes, drained
- ⅓ cup Parmesan cheese, plus more for topping
- ½ cup milk
- Dried parsley, for topping

## Steps
1. Add the frozen meatballs to a crockpot with the jar of Alfredo sauce. Season with the pepper, oregano, thyme, onion powder, garlic powder, smoked paprika, and red pepper flakes.
2. Add the chopped sun-dried tomatoes, Parmesan, and milk. Stir everything well.
3. Cook on low for 4 hours. Stir once in a while if you can.
4. Top with more Parmesan and dried parsley. Serve over mashed potatoes, rice, or pasta.

## Tips
- The meatballs go in straight from the freezer. You don’t need to thaw them first.
- He uses [his homemade meatballs](https://cookinginthemidwest.com/blog/meatball-recipe/) from the freezer. Store-bought Italian-style frozen meatballs work, and he says frozen chicken or turkey meatballs would be good too.
- This has a little heat. To tone it down, leave out the red pepper flakes and black pepper, or add more Alfredo or milk.
- All crockpots cook differently. In his, this is best right at 4 hours on low. He recommends low if you can; high can make the sauce separate or burn. If you do use high, stir now and then and watch the sauce.
- If the sauce separates or gets too thick (from the crockpot running hot, or from oil on the sun-dried tomatoes), stir in a splash of milk right before serving.
- He served these over mashed potatoes with green beans and rolls. For a busy night he likes Bob Evans microwaveable mashed potatoes.
- Leftovers keep in the fridge. Reheat in a pot on the stove over medium heat, adding a splash of milk and stirring until hot.
- Fresh spinach can be stirred in at the end, before serving.
- Short on time: he says you can add everything to a pot or large skillet and cook over medium heat, stirring until the meatballs are hot.
