# One Pot Fajita Chicken Mac N Cheese (DASH)

**Based on:** [[One Pot Fajita Chicken Mac N Cheese]]
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 10 min · **Cook:** 25–30 min

> DASH swaps: the 3 tsp of salt in the two spice sets is gone (that's about 1,700 mg a serving), fat-free evaporated milk, 4 oz of cheese instead of 12, whole-wheat shells, an extra pepper, and black beans for fiber.

## Ingredients

**For the chicken**
- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breast
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp chili powder
- ½ tsp oregano
- ½ tsp cumin
- 1 lime

**Vegetables and pasta**
- 2 bell peppers, sliced
- ½ red onion, sliced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 8 oz whole wheat shell pasta
- 2 cups water
- 1 can (15 oz) no-salt-added black beans, drained and rinsed

**For the cheese sauce**
- 1 can (12 oz) fat-free evaporated milk
- 2 oz sharp cheddar, shredded
- 2 oz pepper jack cheese, shredded
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp chili powder
- ½ tsp oregano
- ½ tsp cumin
- 2 tbsp cilantro, chopped

## Steps
1. Rub the chicken with the oil and the first spice set. Sear until cooked through, then take it out. Squeeze half the lime over it and slice.
2. Cook the peppers and onion in the same pan until soft, then add the garlic.
3. Add the pasta and water (just enough to cover). Simmer about 15 min, until most of the water is gone.
4. Stir in the evaporated milk, cheeses, second spice set, and black beans until creamy.
5. Add the chicken back, squeeze over the rest of the lime, and top with cilantro.

## Tips
- Taste before adding any salt; the cheese brings enough for most people.
- Pickled jalapeños are salty; use fresh jalapeño for heat.
