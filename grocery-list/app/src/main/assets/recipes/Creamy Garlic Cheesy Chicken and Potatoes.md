# Creamy Garlic Cheesy Chicken and Potatoes

**Source:** [Facebook reel](https://www.facebook.com/reel/2019053808776273/) (shared via https://www.facebook.com/share/r/1RR818qtMx/)
**Creator:** Jalalsamfit
**Servings:** 8 (creator’s macros: 536 calories, 52g protein, 44g carbs, 17g fat per serving)
**Prep time:** 20–25 min *(estimate; he doesn’t list a prep time)*
**Cook time:** 45–47 min (20–22 min for the potatoes, then 25 min with the chicken)
**Oven:** 200°C / 400°F
**Pan:** a large lined sheet pan. He says he used a “41 x 36 x 5 inch” pan; that size is huge for inches and is more likely 41 × 36 × 5 cm (about 16 × 14 × 2 inches) *(this is a note, not something he said)*

The full written recipe is in the Facebook caption. Amounts below are his unless marked as an estimate.

## Ingredients

### Chicken & Seasonings
- 800g raw chicken breast, cubed (about 1¾ lb) *(lb conversion is an estimate)*
- 800g raw boneless chicken thighs, cubed (about 1¾ lb) *(lb conversion is an estimate)*
- 3 tsp salt
- 5 tsp Italian herb seasoning
- 4 tsp garlic powder
- 4 tsp onion powder
- 4–5 tsp paprika
- 6 tsp olive oil

### Crispy Roasted Potatoes
- 1600g raw potatoes, cubed (about 3½ lb) *(lb conversion is an estimate)*
- Chopped red onion *(mentioned in the instructions, but no amount is listed; estimate: 1 medium)*
- 3 tsp Italian herb seasoning
- 3 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp chili flakes
- 3 tsp paprika
- Cooking spray, or 3 tsp olive oil

### Creamy Sauce + Garnish
- 400–450ml regular or evaporated milk (about 1¾ cups) *(cup conversion is an estimate)*
- 250g light garlic & herb cream cheese (about 9 oz) *(oz conversion is an estimate)*
- 120g shredded mozzarella or any cheese (about 1 cup / 4 oz) *(cup conversion is an estimate; in the steps he also calls this “a good handful”)*
- Fresh parsley, finely chopped
- Grated Parmesan *(optional)*

## Steps
1. Cube the chicken breast and thighs. Season with the salt, Italian herb seasoning, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, and olive oil, then set aside.
2. Add the cubed potatoes and chopped red onion to a large lined sheet pan. Season with the potato spices and cooking spray (or 3 tsp olive oil). Bake in a 200°C / 400°F preheated oven for 20–22 minutes. They do not need to be fully cooked yet.
3. Spread the seasoned chicken over the potatoes in a single layer, spray with cooking spray, and bake another 25 minutes, until the chicken is cooked and juicy.
4. Take the pan out. Add the milk or evaporated milk, light garlic & herb cream cheese, and a good handful of mozzarella.
5. The remaining heat from the pan will help melt and thicken the sauce. Mix until everything is combined, creamy, and the cheese is melted.
6. Garnish with parsley and optional Parmesan. Divide into 8 servings, or more if you want smaller portions, and enjoy.

## Tips
- This is a sheet-pan meal prep. He says it’s efficient, low effort, and has minimal cleanup.
- Use regular milk or evaporated milk; he lists both.
- The cream cheese is a light garlic & herb style, not a plain block.
- All crockpot-style leftovers aside, divide into 8 (or more) containers depending on your calorie needs. His macros are for 8 servings.
