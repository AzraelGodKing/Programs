# Crockpot Creamy Meatball Pasta

**Source:** [Facebook reel](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1438747121685327/) (shared via https://www.facebook.com/share/r/1EfDrPMdGK/)
**Creator:** Recipes With Scotty
**Servings:** 6 *(estimate)*
**Prep time:** 10 min *(estimate)* to load the crockpot, plus time to boil the pasta later
**Cook time:** 2½ hours on HIGH, plus the pasta cooked according to the package

## Ingredients
- 1 bag Farm Rich Flame Broiled Italian Style Meatballs, frozen
- 1 jar (24 oz) Kroger Marinara Pasta Sauce
- 1 jar Kroger Traditional Alfredo Sauce
- 1 block (8 oz) cream cheese
- 1 tablespoon minced garlic
- garlic salt, to taste
- black pepper, to taste
- garlic powder, to taste
- onion powder, to taste
- Kinder’s Buttery Garlic & Herb seasoning, to taste
- 1 lb bowtie pasta, cooked
- 1 bag (2 cups) shredded mozzarella cheese
- parsley flakes, for topping

## Steps
1. Add the frozen meatballs to the crockpot.
2. Pour the marinara sauce and Alfredo sauce over the meatballs. He used the Kroger brand for both and says any brand is fine.
3. Add the block of cream cheese and the minced garlic.
4. Season with garlic salt, black pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and Kinder’s Buttery Garlic & Herb seasoning. Mix it up and cover.
5. Cook on HIGH for 2½ hours.
6. Cook 1 lb of bowtie pasta according to the package directions and drain.
7. Add the cooked pasta and the full bag of shredded mozzarella to the crockpot. Mix until everything is combined and the cheese is melted and creamy.
8. Top with parsley flakes and serve.

## Tips
- The meatballs go in frozen; there's no need to thaw them first.
- Seasonings are all "to taste." He calls Kinder’s Buttery Garlic & Herb his favorite seasoning in the mix.
