# High-Protein Creamy Parmesan Chicken Pasta (DASH)

**Based on:** [[High-Protein Creamy Parmesan Chicken Pasta]]
**Servings:** 4
**Prep:** 15 min · **Cook:** 20 min

> DASH swaps: a quick homemade Italian dressing (olive oil, red wine vinegar, herbs) instead of bottled dressing, which is the saltiest thing in the original; fresh red pepper instead of jarred; less cream cheese and Parmesan; whole-wheat penne; and spinach.

## Ingredients
- 1¼ lb boneless skinless chicken breast, diced bite-size
- 8 oz whole wheat penne
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- ½ tsp black pepper
- Nonstick cooking spray
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 3 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- ½ cup low-sodium chicken broth
- ½ medium white onion, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 6 oz light cream cheese
- ½ cup grated Parmesan
- 3 cups baby spinach
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

## Steps
1. Boil the penne in unsalted water until al dente. Drain.
2. Toss the chicken with the garlic powder, onion powder, Italian seasoning, and pepper.
3. Spray a large skillet and cook the chicken over medium-high until browned and 165°F, 6–8 min.
4. Add the olive oil, vinegar, garlic, broth, onion, and bell pepper. Simmer until the onion softens, 3–4 min.
5. Lower to medium-low. Stir in the cream cheese until smooth.
6. Stir in the penne, spinach, and Parmesan until the cheese melts and the spinach wilts.
7. Divide into 4 servings and top with parsley.
