# Creamy Chicken & Shrimp Fettuccine Alfredo

## Ingredients
- 2 whole chicken breasts *(shown)*
- A reddish-brown seasoning rubbed on the chicken: salt, pepper, and herbs are visible, likely a Cajun or blackening blend *(the exact blend is a guess)*
- Olive oil, for rubbing the chicken and for the pan *(amount not shown)*
- 8 shrimp, peeled and deveined, tossed in the same blackening seasoning *(8 shrimp are shown already cooked; the seasoning on them is shown)*
- 2–3 tbsp butter *(estimate)*
- About 2 tbsp minced garlic *(estimate)*
- 2 cups heavy cream, poured from a jar *(amount is an estimate; a jar is shown)*
- More of the same seasoning, plus red pepper flakes *(amounts not shown)*
- ¼–½ cup grated Parmesan, plus more for serving *(amount is an estimate)*
- 8 oz fettuccine *(estimate)*
- ¼ cup starchy pasta water *(estimate; a splash is shown)*
- Fresh parsley, for garnish *(shown)*

## Steps
1. Boil the fettuccine in salted water until al dente. Save a splash of the pasta water before you drain it. *(pasta cook time is an estimate from the box; the reel starts with cooked pasta)*
2. Rub the chicken breasts with the seasoning and olive oil. Heat a little oil in the sauté pan over medium to medium-high heat *(heat is an estimate)* and sear the chicken until it’s blackened, then flip and cook the other side. Take the chicken out.
3. Sear the shrimp in the same pan until they’re cooked and blackened, then take them out. *(the reel only shows the finished shrimp; cooking them in this pan after the chicken is an estimate)*
4. Add the butter and minced garlic to the fond left in the pan and cook until fragrant.
5. Pour in the heavy cream. Stir in more of the same seasoning and the red pepper flakes. Add the grated Parmesan and simmer until the sauce is thick.
6. Toss in the cooked fettuccine with a splash of starchy pasta water until the pasta is coated.
7. Slice the chicken if you want, then set it back on top of the pasta with the shrimp. Pour a little extra sauce over the top and finish with Parmesan and parsley.

## Tips
- The official amounts, steps, and any chef’s tips are in Seafood Network’s paid Substack post: [Chicken & Shrimp Fettuccine Alfredo](https://seafoodnetwork.substack.com/p/chicken-and-shrimp-fettuccine-alfredo). Use that if you have access; everything measured here is only an estimate from the video.
- The on-screen title is “Creamy Fettuccine Alfredo.” The Facebook title and caption call it Creamy Chicken & Shrimp Fettuccine Alfredo.
- A homemade Alfredo this way can tighten up fast. Keep a little pasta water handy and loosen the sauce if it gets too thick.
- Don’t overcook the shrimp; they only need a couple of minutes per side *(time is an estimate)*.
