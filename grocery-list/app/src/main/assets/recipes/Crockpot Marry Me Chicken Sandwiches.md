# Crockpot Marry Me Chicken Sandwiches

**Source:** [Facebook reel by Cookinginthemidwest](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1376224050748913/) (shared as https://www.facebook.com/share/r/1Dwr8ZgJPt/)
**Servings:** ~4–6 sandwiches *(estimate)*
**Prep:** ~10 min *(estimate)* · **Cook:** 4–6 hr on LOW, plus ~10 min to melt the cheese *(estimate)*

> The reel has no written recipe. Only the chicken (1 lb), the cream cheese (1 block), and the cook time were stated. Everything else is measured from what's on screen or estimated.

## Ingredients
- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts (2 breasts)
- 2–3 tbsp dry seasoning blend *(estimate; labels not shown. A good mix: 1 tbsp Italian seasoning, 1 tsp paprika, 1 tsp onion powder, ½ tsp salt, ½ tsp black pepper)*
- 1 tsp garlic powder *(estimate; a white powder was added but not labeled)*
- 1 tbsp minced garlic
- ¼–½ tsp red pepper flakes *(estimate; a red sprinkle was added)*
- ⅓ cup chopped oil-packed sun-dried tomatoes *(amount estimated from the video)*
- ¾ cup chicken broth *(estimated; looked like ½–1 cup)*
- 8 oz block cream cheese
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella *(stated as "a handful", estimated)*
- 4–6 brioche buns, toasted
- Cooked bacon, about 2 slices per sandwich
- Butter for toasting the buns *(estimate)*

## Steps
1. Put the chicken in a 6 qt slow cooker. Add the seasonings, minced garlic, red pepper flakes, sun-dried tomatoes, and broth.
2. Cover and cook on LOW for 4–6 hours, until the chicken is 165°F and tender.
3. Shred the chicken right in the pot. She uses a hand mixer on low, which does it in seconds, but two forks work too.
4. Add the cream cheese and mozzarella, put the lid back on, and let it melt about 10–15 min *(estimate)*. Stir until creamy.
5. Butter and toast the buns in a skillet until golden.
6. Pile the chicken on the bottom buns, top with bacon, and close them up.

## Tips
- If it's too soupy after cooking, spoon off some liquid before adding the cheese so the filling doesn't make the buns soggy.
- A little oil from the sun-dried tomato jar adds a lot of flavor.
- In the video it's served with tater tots and broccoli.
