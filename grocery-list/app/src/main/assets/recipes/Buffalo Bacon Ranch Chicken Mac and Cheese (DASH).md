# Buffalo Ranch Chicken Mac and Cheese (DASH)

**Based on:** [[Buffalo Bacon Ranch Chicken Mac and Cheese]]
**Servings:** 4 meal-prep containers
**Prep:** ~15 min · **Cook:** ~3 hr on HIGH or ~5–6 hr on LOW

> DASH swaps: no bacon (smoked paprika gives the smoky note), a buffalo sauce stretched with no-salt-added tomato sauce so it uses far less hot sauce, a homemade salt-free ranch blend instead of the packet, Greek yogurt and light cream cheese for the creaminess, whole-wheat macaroni, less cheddar, and broccoli in the mix.

## Ingredients
- 1¼ lb boneless skinless chicken breast
- 1 can (8 oz) no-salt-added tomato sauce
- 2 tbsp hot sauce (Frank's style)
- 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- ½ cup low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp dried dill
- 1 tsp dried parsley
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 8 oz whole wheat elbow macaroni
- 3 cups broccoli florets
- 2 oz light cream cheese
- ½ cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
- 2 oz sharp cheddar, shredded
- 2 green onions, sliced

## Steps
1. Put the chicken in the slow cooker. Stir together the tomato sauce, hot sauce, vinegar, broth, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, dill, parsley, and pepper, and pour it over.
2. Cover and cook on HIGH about 3 hours (or LOW 5–6 hours), until the chicken hits 165°F and shreds easily.
3. About 20 minutes before it's done, boil the macaroni in unsalted water until just al dente. Add the broccoli for the last 3 minutes. Drain.
4. Shred the chicken in the pot. Turn the cooker off and stir in the cream cheese until it melts, then the Greek yogurt (adding it off the heat keeps it from splitting).
5. Stir in the macaroni, broccoli, and most of the cheddar.
6. Divide into 4 containers and top with the rest of the cheddar, the green onions, and a few drops of hot sauce if you want more heat.

## Tips
- Want it hotter? Add cayenne or red pepper flakes rather than more hot sauce; the sodium is in the sauce.
- Reheat with a splash of milk to loosen the sauce.
- Keeps 4 days in the fridge.
