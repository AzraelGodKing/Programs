# One Pan Chicken Bacon Gnocchi

**Source:** [Facebook reel](https://www.facebook.com/reel/2684096395321396/) (shared via https://www.facebook.com/share/v/14uS6btMaFS/)
**Creator:** Andrea
**Servings:** 4 *(estimate)*
**Prep time:** 15 min *(estimate)*
**Cook time:** 25–30 min *(estimate)*
**Pan:** a large skillet

The Facebook caption is only “The most amazing one pan dinner!” Amounts below are from her voiceover unless marked as an estimate.

## Ingredients
- About 12 oz bacon, chopped small
- ½ cup grated Parmesan, for the breading
- ½ cup Italian-style breadcrumbs
- 2 chicken breasts, split in half to make thin cutlets
- Seasoning for the chicken, whatever you like *(she doesn’t name a blend or give an amount)*
- A little oil from the sun-dried tomato jar *(amount not given; estimate: 1 tbsp)*
- About ¼ cup chopped sun-dried tomatoes *(she says “maybe a fourth of a cup or so”)*
- 1 package gnocchi *(she doesn’t say the size; estimate: 16 oz, a typical package)*
- About 1 tbsp minced garlic
- About ¾ cup chicken broth
- About 1 cup heavy cream
- Smoked paprika, Italian seasoning, and black pepper, to taste *(amounts not given)*
- About 1 cup freshly shredded Parmesan, plus more for topping *(the topping amount is an estimate)*
- A couple cups chopped spinach

## Steps
1. Chop the bacon small and cook it in a large skillet over medium-low heat until it’s crispy.
2. While the bacon cooks, mix the grated Parmesan and Italian-style breadcrumbs in a bowl. Split the chicken breasts in half so they’re thin. Season them however you want, then dip them in the breadcrumb mixture so both sides are well coated.
3. Take the crispy bacon out of the pan and leave the grease. Cook the chicken in that grease for 3 minutes, then flip and cook another 3 minutes, until golden. Remove the chicken.
4. Wipe out some of the black bits, but leave a lot of them in the pan.
5. Add a little of the sun-dried tomato oil and the chopped sun-dried tomatoes. Add the gnocchi and keep tossing until it’s toasted. Stir in the minced garlic and toss again.
6. Pour in the chicken broth and heavy cream. Season with smoked paprika, Italian seasoning, and pepper, however you want.
7. Turn up the heat and stir in about 1 cup freshly shredded Parmesan until it melts. Add the chopped spinach and toss until it wilts.
8. Stir about half of the bacon back in. Top with the chicken, more shredded Parmesan, and the rest of the bacon.

## Tips
- She says this was a big hit with her family.
- The chicken only needs about 3 minutes per side because the cutlets are thin. Don’t overcook it while you wait for the gnocchi.
- Leaving some of the browned bits in the pan after the chicken is what flavors the sauce.
- Use oil-packed sun-dried tomatoes so you have that oil to add to the pan, the way she does.
- A 16 oz package of shelf-stable or refrigerated potato gnocchi is the usual size if the package in the video isn’t marked *(that size is an estimate)*.
