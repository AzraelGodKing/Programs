# Cajun Chicken Alfredo Soup

**Source:** [Facebook reel](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1668331678345031/) (shared via https://www.facebook.com/share/r/1GD6ceWXvr/). Full recipe: [This Is Not Diet Food](https://www.thisisnotdietfood.com/cajun-chicken-alfredo-soup/)
**Creator:** Erin, This Is Not Diet Food
**Servings:** 6
**Prep time:** 15 min
**Cook time:** 25 min (40 min total)
**Pot:** a large Dutch oven (the reel shows a pink enameled one, about 5.5–7 qt)

> The reel is a 14-second montage set to music, so all amounts and steps come from Erin's written recipe on her site.

## Ingredients
- 10 strips bacon, chopped (she used Kirkland fully cooked bacon)

**For the chicken**
- 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast, sliced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning

**For the soup**
- ¼ cup butter
- 2 tbsp chopped garlic (the reel shows Kirkland minced garlic)
- ¼ cup all-purpose flour
- 4 cups chicken broth
- 4 cups half and half
- 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning
- 8 oz noodles (she used fettuccine ricce, broken into bite-size pieces)
- ½ cup shredded Parmesan
- ½ cup shredded mozzarella

## Steps
1. Toss the chicken slices with the olive oil and 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning and set aside.
2. Cook the bacon in the Dutch oven over medium heat until crispy, then move it to a plate lined with paper towels.
3. Cook the chicken in the same pot over medium heat until it's browned on both sides and cooked through, about 8 min. Move it to a cutting board.
4. Melt the butter in the pot. Add the garlic and cook for a couple of minutes.
5. Stir in the flour and cook about 3 min.
6. Add the broth, scraping up the browned bits from the bottom of the pot.
7. Stir in the half and half and 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning, then bring it to a low boil.
8. Add the noodles, broken up if they're long. Stir, drop the heat to medium-low, and simmer, stirring now and then, until tender, about 15 min.
9. Take the pot off the heat and stir in the Parmesan and mozzarella.
10. Chop the chicken into bite-size pieces and add it back. Set aside about 2 tbsp of bacon for topping and stir the rest in.
11. Serve with crusty bread or garlic bread for dipping, which is how it's shown in the reel.

## Tips (from Erin)
- Cajun blends vary in salt and heat, so start low and add more to taste.
- Cook the flour for the full 3 minutes so the soup doesn't taste like raw flour.
- Add the cheese off the heat so the broth doesn't go grainy, and shred it yourself so it melts smoother.
- The pasta keeps soaking up liquid, so loosen leftovers with a splash of broth or half and half. Don't freeze it.
- If you're making it ahead, cook the pasta separately and stir it in just before serving.
- Variations she suggests: chicken thighs or rotisserie chicken, smoked sausage or andouille, cayenne or hot sauce for more heat, or shrimp instead of chicken.
