# High-Protein Buffalo Bacon Ranch Chicken Mac & Cheese

**Source:** [Facebook reel by Samuel Benjamin](https://www.facebook.com/reel/2056193551768401) (shared as https://www.facebook.com/share/r/1EPbLqJM3X/)
**Servings:** 4 meal-prep containers
**Prep:** ~15 min *(estimate)* · **Cook:** ~3 hr on HIGH (per caption), or ~5–6 hr on LOW *(estimate)*
**Per serving (creator's numbers):** ~635 cal · ~61 g protein · ~46 g carbs · ~23 g fat

## Ingredients
- 1½ lb boneless skinless chicken breast (about 3 breasts, left whole)
- 1½ cups buffalo wing sauce (Sweet Baby Ray's in the video), plus extra for topping
- 4 oz reduced-fat cream cheese
- 1 packet ranch seasoning (about 1 oz; looks like Hidden Valley)
- 8 oz high-protein elbow macaroni
- 4 oz shredded sharp cheddar, a little held back for topping
- ¼ cup cooked bacon pieces, a little held back for topping
- 2 green onions, sliced *(amount estimated)*
- Salt for the pasta water *(estimate)*

## Steps
1. Put the whole chicken breasts in a 6–7 qt slow cooker. Add the buffalo sauce, cream cheese, and ranch seasoning on top.
2. Cover and cook on HIGH about 3 hours (or LOW about 5–6 hours), until the chicken hits 165°F and shreds easily.
3. About 20 minutes before the chicken is done, boil the macaroni in salted water until just al dente (check the box, usually 7–9 min *(estimate)*). Drain.
4. Shred the chicken right in the slow cooker with two forks and stir it into the sauce until creamy.
5. Stir in the cooked macaroni, most of the cheddar, and most of the bacon until everything is coated and the cheese melts.
6. Divide evenly into 4 meal-prep containers. Top with the reserved cheddar and bacon, green onions, and a drizzle of buffalo sauce.

## Tips
- The video shows the dial on LOW, while the caption says HIGH for ~3 hours. Either works; LOW just takes longer.
- The video uses a full 8 oz block of regular Philadelphia and what looks like two whole bottles of buffalo sauce. The written recipe (used above) is lighter: 4 oz reduced-fat cream cheese and 1½ cups sauce. Go with the video version for a richer, saucier batch, but the macros will go up.
- Undercook the pasta slightly so it doesn't go mushy when reheated.
- Store in the fridge up to 4 days *(estimate, standard food safety)*; reheat with a splash of water or milk to loosen the sauce.
- Pre-cooked bacon bits or crumbled bacon both work.
