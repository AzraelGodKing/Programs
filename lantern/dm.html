Shop

      
    

    

      

        
        
      

      

      

        
        
      

      
Uncheck a line to take it off this counter. A bare number in the price is gold. Write pp, gp, ep, sp, or cp when you mean those coins: 1 pp is 10 gp, and 1 ep is 5 sp. Players sell these goods back at half that price, and can buy that sale back for the same coin until you close the shop. A service is not bought back.

      

      
Haggling

      
A discount or markup for one player at this counter, in percent: −20 is a fifth off, 25 is a quarter more. Only that player sees it. Open the shop again to apply a change.
